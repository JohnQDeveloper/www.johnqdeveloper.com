## Chapter 2: Just before...

"Finally," Min said as she pulled open the door to her office.

She was my boss at Hayden Credit Union, a straightforward enough business that at least had some ethics in modern America. The interest rates were fair for the credit risk. Customers were treated like human beings. But at the end of the day, even not-for-profit banks needed income and so like any business there was a heirarchy.

She was the branch manager, not there were more than one in this tiny Idaho town, but what mattered to me was she saw me as a person and not an employee to boss around. When my mother took a serious fall that cracked a rib, the fact I needed a month off with no pay had no red tape attached, for the at home recovery period was a non-issue. Shit happened, and Min was willing to let her employees have a life outside of work.

Of course, not all was fair in life, and she was the daughter of one of the board members. But the fact she was actually a good boss caused most people to overlook her nepotism promotion. Not Tom though. That fucker kept calling her the "whore in charge" and claimed she slept with her daddy whenever he was drinking with his buddies at the local bar. But most people understood Tom was a bit sexist and let it slide after hours but the rumor mill at the water cooler ran 24/7 so comments like that still got around.

She closed the door behind me and sighed. "James. I caught Tom entering the woman's bathroom after Sarah. Sarah did not report it but we have hall cameras for a reason in public areas of the bank. If it was not for my little habit of tracking what he is doing when he isn't visible after a woman mentioned his harassment on her exit interview, I wouldn't have known."

I sighed. Tom was a problem but the fact he was insulting Min after hours meant people assumed the two had a problem which means she needed proof to fire the man. That and Tom was the other nepotism hire. His dad was also on the board, and after Tom got let go for excessive force from the Police, needed a job. It was part of the "deal" that put Min in her position which made things even worse.

Politics. A horrible thing but as natural as breathing for the wealthy and political elites.

I looked her in the eyes and said, "That will get him out of the building but your Dad and his dad might just put him on the night shift and rotate the deck chairs. If she does not come forward..."

"I do not give a fuck. That pig forced one woman to quit already. I won't let it be two."

"Alright, alright."

She smiled, "So, if you wouldn't mind sticking around? He doesn't respect women and if its just me he might try to do something stupid. Well, even stupider than following a woman he is harassing into a bathroom. I worry his Daddy getting him this job might think he can do whatever he wants to me."

I nodded. What was I to do? She had already invited me to her brother's wedding as her +1 and I had agreed. When a chinese beauty, even one coming from money, asks your for help, what would Natasha have done? Helped of course!

I nodded. "I'll stay. I'm not going to abandon a damsel in distress. Do you want me to go get him or should we go together?"

She snorted. "You can get him."

Ever the commanding princess. Sure, she was a good boss, but she still wanted others to play fetch for her. Particularly when she was nervous.

---

Hunting Tom down was easy given the security cameras. He had a fixed route he patrolled while the other guy stayed up front and watched. Sure, Tom might take unscheduled breaks to bully his favorite prey, but it kept him easy to find.

I forced a smile when I found him by the locked vault and said, "Tom, the branch manager needs you to fill out some paperwork for a new laptop. Not sure exactly what it is about."

Tom growled, "So she sent you to fetch me? Typical. She can't afford to break a nail opening a door on her own."

I shrugged. There was no point in confronting a man who would hopefully be out of my life in a few minutes. "Whatever, man. Just come with me so we can get this over with and I can go back to Reddit."

Tom chuckled. "Nerd."

"Jock."

Pretending camraderie would get the job done so why not?

The two of us walked over to Min's office, the only truly walled office among the cubicles in the back for the office staff and I closed the door behind us.

Tom looked confused. There was no paperwork on the desk after all.

"So what do I need to sign for this laptop?"

Min looked at me.

I arched an eyebrow. I never claimed to be an honest man, just a decent one.

She sighed and turned her laptop around then hit play of the security camera video. Sure his dad might bend the rules if he could but the evidence was damning.

Tom threw up his hands and said, "Look, if I hook up with a hottie in the bathroom and no one complains, who cares? Its not like I'm her boss like you two. Don't pretend you two haven't been flirting."

I shook my head. Sure, a comment or two and a plus one might sound like dating to someone people but we were just friends. Beautiful chinese princess from money? Out of my league as an IT nerd. That was for sure.

Min glared at him, "One, I know she is not sleeping with you. Two, I know you've been harassing her and before her it was her predecessor who reported you as well. So look, you need to go. We can do this the easy way with you resigning so Daddy can save your ass again. Or I can out you as a sexist predator to the board."

Tom yelled, "You whore! Just because you slept with my dad or your dad or whoever the fuck it was doesn't matter. My dad won't toss me out on my ass when he hears about this!"

Min shouted back, "Just because you call me names doesn't change the fact sexual harassment is grounds for a lawsuit. Daddy's money won't save you when its gone. You think he'll save your ass then? Just quit like a good boy."

"Fuck off! Fire me and I'll just call my Dad."

"You are fired, mister! Now get the fuck out of this bank before I have you escorted out!"

"Cunt."

Tom slammed Min's office door open as he walked out of the bank. His fingers already dialing his father as he muttered, "I'll get that whore for this."

I closed the door gently and turned to her, "You heard him."

She nodded, "Yeah. But when his dad sees the video he is going to realize his son is going to get them both sued for enabling sexual harassment and that'll get him to come around. His dad cares about his money and his public image more than his son. Sure, he wants an heir, but that is all his son is. A warm body to fit the expected spot. His father doesn't really care about him."

I shrugged. "Your funeral. Anyway, I'm gonna go get some work done."

---

A few minutes later words appeared before everyone in the office as all of us stared off into space.

Someone muttered, "Am I going crazy?"

Another growled darkly, "Just like on Royal Road. It is a good thing I read all those System Apocalypse LitRPGs!"

A third started sobbing in clear distress but everyone was too distracted by the glowing words to help them.

**Reality distorts. Time bends. Space contorts. The gods have arrived and the war for souls begins.**

**Please prompt me, as you would an AI, so that we may make your class selection together.**

Fuck.

Natasha's prophecy of doom had arrived.
