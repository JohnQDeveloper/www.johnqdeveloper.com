# Welcome to the Academy

**"WELCOME TO THE SYSTEM..."**

**"YOU HAVE BEEN DESIGNATED AS SERVING THE GODDESS, MISTRESS OF THE HIGH HEAVENS...""**

What the fuck?

**"INTEGRATION COMPLETE. PLEASE INSERT SPHERE."**

What the fuck was the System? Spheres?

A man with massive white wings of fluffy feathers stood at the center of the hall in the middle of two very different figures. The one on the left had horns and was literally on fire yet it never harmed him or the floor beneath him. The one on the right was an asian woman of unclear origins, perhaps Korean would be the closest, dressed in a white kimono with pink flowers of all things. How strange for a presumably korean woman to be wearing japanese clothing.

The angel spoke softly, yet it felt like his words landed directly in my ear despite the fact I was easily thirty rows of recliners and students from him.

"You have recieved access to the System but likely have no clue what that means. The simple answer is until you insert a Sphere, it is useless to you. You have just enough mana or Qi or vital energy or whatever equivalent you believe in. A sphere acts like a generator with a mixture of natural affinity, rank, and sphere type determining exactly how much excess you have to work with and its efficiency with various supernatural abilities."

That would explain why no one on Earth stumbled upon it if it was something artificial, created by the gods. They decided who had access and wether we had a sphere.

"Sphere integration will happen before your first real test at the Academy as we want you to at least understand the basics as both the core skill and sphere are integrated so deeply within a soul it is often lethal to remove them afterwards at a soul level. That means no resurrection, no magic trick to save you. You just are permanently dead, your soul torn asunder. Consuming such souls is how Odium's monsters grow in strength so we strongly advise you to not get eaten or attempt the removal of such things."

Well, that explained the war. If Odium wanted people's souls, it was clear resistance was the only option. No one wanted to be dinner.

"So, Welcome to Foreunner Academy, the System will inform you where you are going and why when you exit the hall. While many of you have recieved a tentative faction designation, the simple fact remains it is a choice you make day after day. The various representatives of each faction are here precisely because they wish to sway you to take a different route. I advise you to stick with your original sponsor. People are often seen as traitors when they switch and never truly trusted. That lack of trust translates to a lack of allies when Odium's monsters come for you."

The angel stepped back after that and the woman on the right took his place in the center of the three lecturers. Her voice was like music to the ears as she spoke, "The Cycle welcomes all because it is the natural state of things for souls to be washed clean and granted new flesh so that they might improve their karma. A person forced into crime because of circumstance should be given a chance at redemption even if it is as a lowly dog. Such is the cycle of reincarnation and the nature of the gods no matter what alternative philosophy they might speak now."

"Our method is the original form of growing in power, cultivation, before the artificial foundation granted by the System allowed an acceleration of this to enable us to sustain our losses in the Great War. Death at the hands of Odium's monsters is permanent and we thank each and every one of you for your service in defending the multitudes of races against that evil. Look around, you will see humans, orcs, demons, and a dozen other kinds of humanoids. These are all equal in the eyes of the gods as long as they strive to reach the Heavens."

"With us, unlike the Demons or the High Heavens, you are guarenteed a new life should you die of a cause other than Odium. A life to try again without concern for meeting the standards of the High Heavens or needing to pay the cost of a flawed form of immortality as used by the Demons. With us, your soul and its future is secure."

She stepped to the right and the flaming man with black skin and red runes now stared up at the row upon row of new students.

The demons voice was rough like gravel as he spoke. Odd for a creature of the flame but perhaps there was more to it than that.

"Demons are pragmatic. For those who practice capitalism, we practice it in its purest form where even immortality can be bought for the right price. While the Cycle spouts its nonsense about reincarnation, they leave out your memories will be wiped and what are we but a collection of our own memories?"

"The High Heavens derides traitors and has steep penalties for anyone daring to leave their ranks. Yet, if they were truly so wonderful, why would anyone leave? Oh right, because if you do not conform your soul endles up who knows where. Each soul is teleported in front of closed gates but where do they go? You have only their word it is to a better place. Why not show it to everyone if it was truly a better place?"

"So the offer of the Demons is harsh but fair. We leave our doors open to all comers. You just have to earn your keep and your immortality with the blood of our enemies. Your memories will never be wiped. You do not need to trust in snake oil promises you cannot verify."

"Every one of you has heard of Vampires and Werewolves and others of their ilk. Beings who are truly immortal. We offer everyone that opportunity with the spheres of Blood and Shifting. You just need to afford the cost of the skill required to complete the transformation. Even the Sphere of Knowledge can create a Lich who will live forever. Or the Sphere of Influence can create an Incubus. There are as many roads to immortality as there are Spheres."

"This is the only life you have. Why risk it on snake oil salesmen who make promises they do not provide proof of?"

With that the demon moved aside so that the angelic being could take the center once more. His voice was soft and calm, likely used to the demon's provocations, "With that, our presentation is concluded. You are free to seek us out our offices within the Academy grounds if you wish the System to change your faction. But like I said, someone sponsored you to be here and you should honor that commitment and a chance to extend your life beyond what was normal and natural."

"Despite what the demonic factions attempt to cast doubt, the Cycle and the High Heavens have never had anyone complain about our us failing to honor our commitments."

**"WELCOME TO THE FORERUNNER ACADEMY, STUDENT."**

**"PLEASE EXIT THE LECTURE HALL ON THE LEFT AND PROCEED TO BUILDING H3, DORM 13. DIRECTIONS WILL BE PROVIDED IF REQUESTED."**
