### Chapter 3: The Shit Hits The Fan

Who did I want to be in this new world? The technical wizard? The doomsday prepper? Or the hero?

The truth was deep down I just wanted to protect people, enjoy the company of a few beautiful women, and matter to people. As much as I might hate politics, I still wanted to be someone important at the end of the day. Someone who mattered. Someone who could eat real food instead of ramen on the days before the paycheck cleared.

I thought at the AI, since I had no idea how this worked, that concept. A defender, a prepper, a technical wizard, a hero.

**Input accepted. Tutorial class options are Artificer, Guardian, Hero, Survivalist.**

**Hero is a generic class, like Survivor and similar, built around helping others before yourself.**

**Artificer is a specialist in the creatione of physical objects of System Wizardry. Not quite a wizard but impossible to beat if you can prepare.**

**Defender is a forward positioned protector of those who stand behind him. A bulwark against the tide of the Void and Odium's monsters.**

**Survivalist is one who prepared the ground long before the fight began. Traps, hunting, these were his skills and he was good at them.**

**System Adjusting.... High Heavens signet ring detected....**

**Faction Class Unlock: Sanctuary Builder.**

**The Sanctuary Builder is the shield that guards the realms of men. However, you must have a home or a sanctuary stone upon which to build. This class is a hybrid of the Artificer and the Defender. A bulwark of magical might against the Darkness.**

**Be aware, you have limited skill slots. A hybrid class will suffer severe skill pressure but is more likely to be unique in outcomes. Unique could be good, or bad. It depends on your tactical and strategic thinking when choosing which skills to keep and which to toss.**

Warning or not, the Sanctuary Builder is what I wanted. It was a class for a nerd investigating the System's secrets but also a protector of other people. Besides, who would ever turn down being able to use magic?

If my thoughts brought out the answer, then I organized my thoughts and protected the decision at the AI.

**Sanctuary Builder selected.**

**Initial skill unlock: Bash - You can take a blunt instrument, such as a club or shield, and bash someone's head in despite their armor.**

**Initial skill unlock: Artifice - You have insight into System Wizardry. However, blueprints must be acquired to use.**
